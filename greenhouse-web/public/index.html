Smart greenhouse monitoring

How the system works

Every reading travels the same path, and every screen reads from the database, never straight from the device.

SensorsTemperature & humidity, soil moisture, light
Arduino / ESP32Decides locally: fan, pump, light. Works even if Wi-Fi drops.
Web backendValidates data, raises alerts, enforces who may see what.
DatabaseTimestamped readings, settings, alerts, activity.
Dashboard & AICharts, history, and plain-language analysis.

What it does

Controls by itself

  • Fan cools when it gets too warm
  • Pump waters when the soil is dry
  • Light follows a schedule
IDLECOOLINGWATERINGFAULT

Fails safe

  • Sensor failure puts the device in FAULT and stops the actuators
  • Lost connection: it keeps its last settings
  • Stale or missing data is clearly marked, never hidden

Remote settings

  • Change fan and pump thresholds from the site
  • See whether the device confirmed the change
  • AI suggestions are only suggestions: you review them first

For growers

  • Your own greenhouse, your own data
  • Charts and history for any date range
  • Alerts when something goes wrong

For administrators

  • All users, greenhouses and assignments
  • System-wide monitoring and comparison
  • Alert rules and activity record

Honest AI

  • The server calculates the numbers
  • AI only interprets them
  • Facts, statistics and AI opinion are labelled separately
